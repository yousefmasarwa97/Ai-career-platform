TL;DR
HasoubLabs wants to build a unified, multi-agent AI platform that empowers HasoubLabs community candidates to access and secure high-impact technology jobs.
The platform should support candidates throughout their career journey by using multiple specialized AI agents that collaborate within one enterprise platform. The exact responsibilities of the agents, target users, data sources, employer involvement, and definition of success must be clarified with the client before finalizing the product requirements.
The intended outcome is to reduce the gap between qualified community candidates and suitable high-impact technology opportunities.
Problem Statement
Technology job seekers often need to perform many disconnected activities during their job search:
Finding relevant job opportunities.
Understanding which jobs match their skills.
Improving their CV or professional profile.
Preparing for interviews.
Identifying skill gaps.
Applying for positions.
Tracking applications and progress.
These activities may currently require multiple tools, websites, and manual processes.
The proposed product aims to create a unified AI-powered platform where multiple specialized agents work together to support HasoubLabs community candidates throughout the job-search and career-development process.
Problem to validate
We still need to confirm:
What is the biggest problem HasoubLabs wants to solve?
Who experiences this problem most strongly?
What evidence does HasoubLabs already have that this problem exists?
What existing processes or tools are candidates currently using?

Primary Goals
Goal 1: Create a unified candidate experience
Enable candidates to access the relevant AI-powered career services through one platform instead of using disconnected tools.
Success indicator: TBD based on confirmed user flows and adoption targets.
Goal 2: Improve candidate-to-job matching
Use AI agents and candidate data to identify relevant job opportunities and improve the quality of candidate-job matching.
Success indicator: TBD — for example, match relevance, application conversion, interview invitations, or successful placements.
Goal 3: Provide personalized career support
Enable candidates to receive personalized recommendations based on their:

Goal 4: Demonstrate multi-agent collaboration
Build an architecture where specialized AI agents can perform separate responsibilities while sharing relevant information and coordinating through a unified platform.

Replace human recruiters or career coaches completely.

# Product Requirements Update — Three User Faces

## 1. User Roles

The platform will have three user faces:

### 1.1 Candidate

Candidates are users who are looking for technology job opportunities.

#### Candidate capabilities
- Register and log in securely.
- Create and maintain their own professional profile.
- Upload their CV/resume.
- Store **at most 5 CV versions** per candidate.
- Manage their own CV versions.
- View available job offers.
- Apply to jobs that are available on the platform.
- Receive AI-powered career support and recommendations according to the finalized product scope.

#### Candidate privacy and access restrictions
- A candidate **MUST NOT** be able to view another candidate's profile.
- A candidate **MUST NOT** be able to view another candidate's CV.
- Candidate-facing job discovery is limited to available job offers.
- Candidate private information and CVs must be protected through authorization and role-based access control.

---

### 1.2 Career Mentor

Career Mentors are users who support candidates and can contribute job opportunities to the platform.

#### Career Mentor capabilities
- Register and log in securely.
- Upload job offers so that candidates can discover and apply to them.
- Manage the job offers they have uploaded, subject to the finalized permissions and workflow.
- Optionally provide their personal contact information.
- A Career Mentor **MAY choose not to provide personal contact information**.

#### Career Mentor privacy
- Personal contact information is optional and should only be stored/displayed according to the final privacy and visibility rules.
- Career Mentors do not automatically receive access to all candidate profiles or CVs. Candidate access must follow the finalized authorization rules.

---

### 1.3 Admin

Admins are authorized HasoubLabs platform administrators.

#### Admin capabilities
- View all candidates.
- View candidate profiles.
- View candidate CVs, including the candidate's available CV versions.
- View all job offers.
- Upload job offers.
- Manage/administer platform job offers according to the finalized workflow.
- Ask an AI agent to assist with reviewing a candidate's CV.
- Receive an AI-generated CV review containing relevant career-related observations and recommendations.

#### Admin AI-assisted CV review
The Admin can select a candidate/CV and request an AI-powered review. The AI review should assist the Admin rather than replace the Admin's decision.

The review may include, subject to final implementation:
- Candidate skills identified from the CV.
- Relevant experience.
- Strengths.
- Missing or unclear information.
- Potential improvements.
- Relevant job/profile alignment.
- Other career-related insights supported by the available candidate data.

The system should clearly distinguish AI-generated observations from human/admin decisions.

# 2. Role-Based Access Control

The platform MUST implement role-based authorization for the three user faces.

| Capability | Candidate | Career Mentor | Admin |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| View own profile | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Edit own profile | Yes | Yes | According to admin permissions |
| Upload CV | Yes | No | No |
| Store up to 5 CV versions | Yes | No | View candidate versions |
| View other candidates' profiles | No | Not automatically | Yes |
| View other candidates' CVs | No | Not automatically | Yes |

Because Admin users can access candidate profiles and CVs, while Candidates cannot access other candidates' information, authorization is a critical platform requirement.

The system SHOULD implement:
- Role-based access control (RBAC).
- Server-side authorization for every protected candidate/profile/CV operation.

- Appropriate access logging for sensitive candidate data.

# 6. Updated Multi-Agent Architecture Implications

The proposed multi-agent architecture remains based on a central Orchestrator/Supervisor and specialized agents.

The three user faces change how the agents are invoked:

### Candidate-facing AI
The Orchestrator can route candidate requests to:
- Candidate Profile Agent
- CV Analysis Agent
- Job Matching Agent
- Career Coach Agent
- Interview Agent
- Recommendation Agent

### Admin-facing AI
The Admin can explicitly request an AI-powered CV review. The Orchestrator can route the request to the CV Analysis Agent and, when relevant, other agents such as the Job Matching or Recommendation Agent.

The AI layer MUST respect the user's role and authorization context. An AI agent must not expose candidate information to a user who is not authorized to access that information.

### Career Mentor-facing functionality
Career Mentors primarily interact with job-management functionality. AI assistance for Career Mentors is not required by the current three-role definition unless added later.

# What I Understand About the Project

## Core Vision
The platform is a unified, multi-agent AI career platform connecting **HasoubLabs community candidates → a unified multi-agent enterprise platform → high-impact technology jobs**. Its purpose is to reduce the gap between qualified community candidates and suitable technology opportunities. The PRD describes it as an enterprise-oriented platform rather than a small standalone AI prototype.

## Three User Faces

### Candidates
Candidates use the platform to build their professional profile, upload up to **5 CV versions**, receive AI-powered career support, discover jobs, and apply to them.

A candidate **cannot see another candidate's profile or CV**. Their job-facing experience is focused on available opportunities and their own career information.

### Career Mentors
Career Mentors support the ecosystem primarily by **uploading job offers** for candidates to discover and apply to.

They may provide personal/professional contact information, but doing so is **optional**.

### Admin
Admins have the broadest access. They can see **all candidates, candidate profiles, CV versions, and jobs**. They can also upload job offers.

An Admin can select a candidate's CV and **ask an AI agent to review it**, with the AI acting as decision support rather than replacing the human/Admin decision.

## Candidate Journey
The main candidate journey is:

**Create account → Complete profile → Upload CV (up to 5 versions) → AI analyzes profile/CV → Discover jobs → Apply → Receive career recommendations/assistance → Update profile/CV as needed.**

This extends the original PRD flow, which defines account creation, profile completion, CV upload, AI analysis, job matching, career recommendations, dashboard recommendations, and continued AI assistance. fileciteturn0file0L385-L400

## Proposed Agents
The PRD proposes a central **Orchestrator / Supervisor Agent** coordinating specialized agents:

1. **Candidate Profile Agent** — understands candidate data and extracts skills/experience.
2. **CV Analysis Agent** — analyzes CVs, identifies strengths, missing information, and improvements.
3. **Job Matching Agent** — compares candidate profiles with job requirements.
4. **Career Coach Agent** — identifies skill gaps and suggests career next steps.
5. **Interview Agent** — generates questions, simulates interviews, and provides feedback.
6. **Recommendation Agent** — combines information and prioritizes personalized actions.

The PRD explicitly states that this is a **proposed architecture rather than a confirmed HasoubLabs requirement**. fileciteturn0file0L295-L336
